# Linux PDF apps: recurring complaints about form filling and signing

Method note (read first): about 15 tool rounds were used. **Reddit (www/old.reddit.com) and AUR (aur.archlinux.org, behind Anubis bot protection) could not be fetched**, and search returned no Reddit threads. Flathub web pages don't show user reviews; GNOME Software/ODRS reviews and Snap Store reviews were not reached. So the evidence comes mostly from bug trackers (bugs.kde.org, GNOME Bugzilla), distro forums (openSUSE, Manjaro, Arch, Linux Mint), Hacker News, Lemmy, and project READMEs. No star-rating counts were gathered. Where counts appear, they come from bug-tracker metadata (duplicates or CCs), not from a sample of reviews. Sioyek, Zathura and PDF Studio got no specific complaint evidence in this pass.

## What are the low-star reasons on Flathub, Snap Store, KDE/GNOME listings and AUR comments?

### Takeaway
No direct store-review data could be collected: Flathub doesn't show reviews on the web, and AUR and Reddit blocked automated fetches. The closest stand-ins are bug trackers and forums. In those, the main Okular/Evince/Papers complaints are signing (NSS and smartcard setup, PIN loops, signatures that diverge from Acrobat), form data that does not survive saving or sharing, and XFA forms not supported at all.

### Inferences
- Because Flathub and Snap show no visible reviews, the "low-star" signal for Linux PDF tools lives in bug trackers and forum threads. Duplicate and CC counts (such as 12 dups / 27 CCs on XFA) are the best available stand-in for frequency.
- Many Okular and Evince form and signature bugs end up "RESOLVED UPSTREAM" (Poppler). From the user's side, the app just doesn't work, while the maintainers bounce the ticket to the library.

### Gaps
- No Flathub, Snap Store, GNOME Software (ODRS) or AUR comment text was collected, because those pages were blocked or don't show reviews. Star distributions are unknown.
- No complaint evidence was gathered for Sioyek, Zathura or PDF Studio (only vendor documentation for PDF Studio smartcard setup).

## What do Reddit, Ask Ubuntu, Unix SE, HN and forum threads say is broken or missing for filling and signing PDFs?

### Takeaway
There are three recurring pain clusters. (1) **Forms:** XFA government forms don't work at all; AcroForm data gets lost or isn't readable in Acrobat; flat or scanned forms push users to overlay tools that destroy the real fields. (2) **Signing:** NSS database setup (often via the Firefox profile), PKCS#11 module registration, PIN prompts that loop endlessly with smartcards and national eIDs, and signatures judged differently by Okular/Poppler and by Acrobat. (3) **Fallbacks:** print-sign-scan, Adobe Reader in Wine, dual-booting Windows, browser PDF viewers, and Java/CLI signers.

### Cited Findings
**(a) Form-filling pain**
- The pdf-sign-and-fill README (a Linux tool built to fill this gap) says: "The standard Linux PDF tools — Okular, Evince, Xreader, qpdfview — are excellent readers … but don't reliably fill AcroForm fields or support easily typing/stamping freeform overlays onto a page." (undated) — [github.com/science/pdf-sign-and-fill](https://github.com/science/pdf-sign-and-fill)
- **Adding a signature image strips form fields** (Arch forum, 2020, older): "any change to the PDF file seems to disable or remove the form fields." The poster tried Xournal++, LibreOffice Draw and GIMP. python-signpdf also "removed the form fields." No solution was found. — [bbs.archlinux.org id=254007](https://bbs.archlinux.org/viewtopic.php?id=254007)
- **Xournal++ and checkboxes:** "I tried on Atril, evince xournal++, no pdf tools sees the checkboxes." — [Launchpad ubuntu-mate 1970913](https://bugs.qastaging.launchpad.net/ubuntu-mate/+bug/1970913). The old Xournal tracker says export re-renders some PDFs through printing and loses form fields, and the maintainer didn't know how to fix it — [sourceforge xournal bug 206](https://sourceforge.net/p/xournal/bugs/206)
- **Common workaround for flat forms:** Xournal++ text tool typed over the page (this doesn't keep the real fields) — [Baeldung](https://www.baeldung.com/linux/fill-and-sign-pdfs), [mark.mcnally.je blog](https://mark.mcnally.je/blog/post/Fill%20in%20PDF%20forms%20with%20ease%20using%20Xournal++)
- **LibreOffice Draw as a PDF "editor":** "if I try to use Libre Office Draw I loose the format of my pdf" (Mint forum, 2023-05-09; text left the page and overlapped) — [forums.linuxmint.com t=394059](https://forums.linuxmint.com/viewtopic.php?t=394059). LibreOffice bug trackers: Draw doesn't really "edit" a PDF but imports it into drawing objects. It can't use embedded subset fonts, so text splits into many boxes, justified text is lost, and form fields and links often don't survive. — [ask.libreoffice.org formatting](https://ask.libreoffice.org/t/libreoffice-draw-does-not-save-the-formatting-of-imported-pdfs/58525), [LO bug 153888](https://www.libreoffice.org/bugzilla/show_bug.cgi?id=153888), [ask.libreoffice justified](https://ask.libreoffice.org/t/libreoffice-draw-cant-keep-justified-text-alignment-when-opening-pdf/45510), [ask.libreoffice misaligned](https://ask.libreoffice.org/t/pdfs-are-misaligned-in-draw/59879)
- **HN, Okular forms** (2020, older): "Okular can 'edit' forms. I have been doing this on Linux and Windows for a while." The commenter called it workable but not very usable and fell back to GIMP. — [news.ycombinator.com 24559546](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=24559546)

**(b) Digital signing pain**
- **Smartcard PIN loop in Okular** (KDE bug 459173, opened 2022, still REPORTED, 5 CCs): "okular keeps asking for the password in an infinite loop, without actually applying a signature." 2023: "the okular window enters a 'not responding' state." 2023, Belgian eID: works but "the PIN has to be entered twice." 2024: the loop goes away only after the reporter deletes the non-repudiation certificate from the card. 2025: the same hang on Windows 11. 2026 (John Scott): the cause may be duplicate NSS nicknames, check with `certutil -K`. See-also links: Luxtrust hang (483678) and "Okular Qt6 loops PIN dialog with DNIe" (514887, Spanish eID). — [bugs.kde.org 459173](https://bugs.kde.org/show_bug.cgi?id=459173)
- **Luxtrust non-repudiation cert hangs:** the cause was an NSS lock deadlock because of the double PIN; the reporter had to patch NSS — [okular-devel 483678](https://www.mail-archive.com/okular-devel@kde.org/msg45000.html)
- **openSUSE user, 2023:** "okular goes into an infinite loop wanting the pin of my smart card again and again"; after upgrading to Leap 15.5, "the connection to the card reader does not work anymore"; "So two steps further, but still no success."; "No reply at all, am I in the wrong forum?" — [forums.opensuse.org 165517](https://forums.opensuse.org/t/digital-signature-with-okular/165517)
- **NSS setup friction:** Okular reads certificates only from an NSS database (Firefox profile → /etc/pki/nssdb → ~/.pki/nssdb), so you set it under Settings > Configure Backends > PDF and register the token with `modutil`. The repeated "PIN" prompts turned out to be asking for the Firefox master password. Signatures are visible only, and you draw a rectangle by hand. — [gregbur.me deep dive (2022)](https://gregbur.me/2022/04/28/deep-dive-digitally-signing-pdfs-with-okular/), [rajeeshknambiar (2022)](https://rajeeshknambiar.wordpress.com/2022/06/27/digitally-signing-pdf-documents-in-linux-with-hardware-token-okular/), [KDE Discuss (Windows)](https://discuss.kde.org/t/okular-for-windows-11-pkcs-certificates-not-detected/27783)
- **Lemmy, ~2025 ("Suggestions for Linux PDF Editor and E-sign", c/linux, legal user):** "The problem with Foxit is that it doesn't run natively on Linux" (they run it in WINE). The one reply: Okular works only after you create the certificate in Adobe, save it to a file, register it with NSS, and set NSS as Okular's backend. — [lemmy.world via scribe.disroot.org](https://scribe.disroot.org/post/6747621/8973941)
- **Okular vs Acrobat interop:**
  - 461371: "PDFs digitally signed with Okular become locked in Adobe Acrobat so no one else can sign it." The reporter: "I would not be able to share signed documents with my colleagues who use non-open source tools." (2022; fixed in Poppler 23.05) — [bugs.kde.org 461371](https://bugs.kde.org/show_bug.cgi?id=461371)
  - 447885: a signature valid in Adobe and Autofirma shows as not valid in Okular (2022) — [bugs.kde.org 447885](https://bugs.kde.org/show_bug.cgi?id=447885)
  - 496490: "Okular 'corrupts' (QES-)signed documents." Correct QES signatures show as "changed after signature" while Acrobat says valid (2024, REPORTED; the developer later suspected the PDF-Over signer's padding) — [bugs.kde.org 496490](https://bugs.kde.org/show_bug.cgi?id=496490); also an openSUSE bug 1233440 — [lists.opensuse.org](https://lists.opensuse.org/archives/list/bugs@lists.opensuse.org/thread/TPHUL2YM35AF2K4EW3LVRCFC6CLV6LHA)
  - 482682: Okular signature not referenced in AcroForm (2024) — [bugs.kde.org 482682](https://bugs.kde.org/show_bug.cgi?id=482682)
  - 520947: "A signed pdf is not considered as signed" (June 2026) — [okular-devel 2026-06](https://mail.kde.org/pipermail/okular-devel/2026-June/049401.html)

### Inferences
- Rough frequency, judged by how often each comes up across ~20 distinct sources (not a statistical sample): smartcard/NSS signing friction is the most-repeated signing theme (≥6 independent threads or bugs). XFA is the most-duplicated forms ticket (12 dups). Losing fields or data on save/edit comes up in ≥8 Okular bugs plus the Arch and Xournal threads. Okular–Acrobat signature divergence shows up in ≥5 bugs from 2022–2026.
- National-eID users (Belgium, Luxembourg, Spain DNIe, Austria QES/PDF-Over) are a distinct, motivated segment. Their cards carry two certificates and need double PINs, and Okular/NSS handles that badly.
- Compatibility with what the recipient sees in Acrobat is the real acceptance test. Users fear that signed or filled output "isn't readable by Adobe".

### Gaps
- No Reddit, Ask Ubuntu or Unix SE thread text was retrieved (Reddit blocked; search returned no AU/U&L hits). Claims about those communities can't be backed here.
- No recent (2023–2026) HN "what do you use to sign PDFs on Linux" thread was found.

## Complaints about Master PDF Editor (paid, watermark, licence/pricing changes)

### Takeaway
The recurring complaint is that the free Linux build moved from "free for non-commercial use" (v4, up to 4.3.89) to a demo that watermarks any edited output. Users keep hunting for the old v4 builds, and the download links break.

### Cited Findings
- "Master PDF Editor is proprietary software, but there is a demo version it is free to use." / "the demo version inserts a watermark on edited PDFs." (BenTrabetere, 2023-05-12) — [forums.linuxmint.com t=394059](https://forums.linuxmint.com/viewtopic.php?t=394059)
- "I can confirm both for watermark on latest versions and 4.3.89 release is the latest version doesn't insert a watermark." (markinson, 2023-05-13); "The links for pdf-editor-4 do not work." (diabolicbg, 2023-03-26); "Just because it is old doesn't mean it does not work." (bin, 2023-05-13) — same thread
- Older reviews describe the Linux version as free for non-commercial use, with a premium licence at $69.95. AlternativeTo lists a perpetual licence at about $70–85 plus a limited free version. — [how2shout review](https://www.how2shout.com/review/master-pdf-editor-review-linux-windows-macos.html), [alternativeto](https://alternativeto.net/software/master-pdf-editor/about)
- A competitor blog (oxygenpdf, 2026, treat with caution) says editing text, inserting images and most content tools now watermark output until you buy a licence. — [oxygenpdf.com](https://oxygenpdf.com/blog/open-source-pdf-editor)

### Inferences
- The watermark-on-save model feels like bait to Linux users who remember the free v4. It drives them to ancient builds, which is a security and compatibility risk.

### Gaps
- AUR comments for masterpdfeditor (likely the richest source on Qt/packaging breakage and licence complaints) were blocked by Anubis. Exact current pricing was not verified on code-industry.net.

## What would users pay for / what's their ideal tool?

### Takeaway
There is little direct "I'd pay X" evidence. Revealed preferences point to a native Linux app that fills AcroForms and flat forms without destroying fields, signs with smartcards/eIDs out of the box (no NSS or Firefox-profile wiring), produces signatures Acrobat accepts, and needs no Wine. People already pay or put up with friction for Foxit-in-Wine, Master PDF Editor and PDF Studio to get this.

### Cited Findings
- Legal-practice user: needs software that works "out of the box"; the complaint is that Foxit "doesn't run natively on Linux" — [scribe.disroot.org/post/6747621](https://scribe.disroot.org/post/6747621/8973941)
- Interop is the deciding requirement: "I would not be able to share signed documents with my colleagues who use non-open source tools." — [bugs.kde.org 461371](https://bugs.kde.org/show_bug.cgi?id=461371)
- XFA users put it as a choice between Linux and Windows: "forces me to reboot into windows again and use Adobe Reader" — [bugs.kde.org 263042](https://bugs.kde.org/show_bug.cgi?id=263042); "about the only reason I still dual boot Windows" — [HN 13379493](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=13379493)
- Even the open-source maintainers frame XFA as a funding problem ("a few hundred thousands euros") — [bugs.kde.org 263042](https://bugs.kde.org/show_bug.cgi?id=263042)
- Free online signing was rejected for limited legal value, and Skribble for lacking eID — [forum.manjaro.org 28519](https://forum.manjaro.org/t/linux-pdf-signing-software/28519)
- What's paid for today: Master PDF Editor perpetual licence at about $70–85 — [alternativeto](https://alternativeto.net/software/master-pdf-editor/about); PDF Studio is marketed for Linux smartcard/CAC signing — [PDF Studio KB](https://kbpdfstudio.qoppa.com/signing-pdfs-with-cac-under-linux/)

### Inferences
- The most willing payers seem to be professionals (legal, government-facing, EU eID/QES users) for whom signature validity in Acrobat and eID support are mandatory. A one-time perpetual price near the Master PDF Editor range ($70–85) is the reference point Linux users already know.
- Differentiators that come straight out of these complaints: built-in PKCS#11/eID detection that handles two certificates per card and a single PIN prompt; a self-check that the signature validates in Acrobat; filling that never drops fields; overlay or "type anywhere" for flat forms; a signature image that keeps the fields; and an honest statement about XFA (detect it and explain, at minimum).

### Gaps
- No survey or explicit willingness-to-pay statements were found. Reddit "I'd pay for…" threads were not reachable.
